编辑目标：`archive:tarot-v4/01-core-sun.png`

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: final production tarot card back image for the Aster plugin library.
Input image 1 is the sole EDIT TARGET, an existing portrait card. Preserve the exact composition, canvas proportions, full card outline, Roman numeral I, ivory border, wine burgundy color, gold sun and clouds, and all illustration details.
Change ONLY the bottom gold ribbon and its word "Core". Enlarge the entire gold scroll ribbon approximately 25% in width and 30% in height, anchored near the same lower edge, expanding upward into the ample empty burgundy area. Enlarge the elegant dark brown calligraphic English word "Core" approximately 25%, centered with ample interior padding from both curled ends. Ribbon must stay entirely INSIDE the card border, centered horizontally; do not cover the illustration above it. Keep the simple flat fills and existing gold edge treatment; no added ornament, no new texture, no gemstones, no extra labels.
Keep exact text: top "I", bottom "Core". One complete card only, no mockup, no perspective change, no extra background. Maintain the original white margin and original output size if possible.
```
